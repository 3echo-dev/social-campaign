# Playbook: voice

The words no draft uses, in one place. `brand-check` enforces this list; `write-caption`
and `write-script` avoid it while writing. It used to exist in three overlapping copies,
which meant a word could be banned in a caption and allowed in a script.

A brand's own `brand/brand-voice.md` sits above this file. Where they disagree the brand
file wins: a word a brand deliberately owns is not an AI tell for that brand. Record that
once and stop flagging it.

## Tier 1A, a frequency marker

Always replace. Three or more in one caption is a High finding on its own.
Match inflected forms.

delve, landscape (metaphor), tapestry, realm, paradigm, embark, beacon, testament to, robust, comprehensive, cutting-edge, leverage (verb), pivotal, underscores, meticulous, seamless, game-changer, hits different, watershed moment, marking a pivotal moment, the future looks bright, only time will tell, nestled, vibrant, thriving, despite challenges continues to thrive, showcasing, deep dive, unpack, bustling, intricate, complexities, ever-evolving, enduring, daunting, holistic, actionable, impactful, learnings, thought leader, best practices, at its core, synergy, interplay, keen (intensifier), genuinely (intensifier), symphony (metaphor), embrace (metaphor), load-bearing (metaphor).

## Tier 1B, a clarity edit

Always replace. Never counts toward the cluster signal.

utilize, in order to, due to the fact that, serves as, features (verb), boasts, presents (inflated), commence, ascertain, endeavor.

## Marketing filler

Refused in captions, spoken lines and on-screen text: game-changer, revolutionary,
best-in-class, unlock, elevate, journey, "it's not just X, it's Y", "we're excited to
announce", "don't miss out", "act now".

## Generation prompt filler

Refused in voice notes and image or video prompts, because they describe a mood rather
than a shot and the model fills the gap with its own: cinematic, professional, stunning,
8k, studio, perfect.

## Punctuation

No em dash, en dash or double dash anywhere in a caption, on-screen text or spoken line.
Plain dash only.
