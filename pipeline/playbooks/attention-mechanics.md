# Attention mechanics

The hook stops the scroll; the mechanic keeps people watching after it. Every concept and post carries one `mechanic_id`: the main thing that holds attention through the middle of the piece.

## Mechanics

| Id (`mechanic_id`) | What it does | Works with | Guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| M-OPEN-LOOP | Raises a question early and answers it at the end | H-CURIOSITY, H-STORY | The answer comes inside the piece |
| M-COUNTDOWN | Numbered steps or items counting down | H-VALUE, H-UTILITY | The count matches what is shown |
| M-LIST-REVEAL | Items revealed one at a time | H-VALUE, H-UTILITY | Each item earns its place |
| M-LIVE-DEMO | The thing is shown happening in real time | H-PROOF | No cuts that hide the result |
| M-PATTERN-BREAK | A sharp change around 3 s (cut, sound, angle) to reset attention | any | One break, not a strobe |
| M-STAKES-RISE | Each beat raises what is at stake | H-STORY, H-PROOF | Stakes stay true to the brand's claims |
| M-REVEAL | The result or answer is held back, then shown | H-CURIOSITY, H-PROOF | The reveal is worth the wait |
| M-COMPARE | Two options shown against each other beat by beat | H-CONTRARIAN, A-COMPARISON | Fair to both sides |
| M-PROOF-STACK | Several proofs in quick succession | H-PROOF | Every proof has provenance |
| M-CALLBACK | The ending returns to the opening line or image | H-STORY, H-CURIOSITY | The callback adds something |

## Rules

1. One main mechanic per piece. Others may appear, but name the one carrying the middle.
2. Static images and single-slide posts use `M-NONE`.
3. A carousel's mechanic is how the slides lead into each other, usually M-LIST-REVEAL or M-OPEN-LOOP.
