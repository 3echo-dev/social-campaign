# Angles

The angle is the offer told through one strategy. The brief still writes the angle as one specific sentence; `angle_id` names which strategy that sentence uses, so pieces can be compared later. Pick the angle that fits the awareness stage (see `awareness-stages.md`).

## Angles

| Id (`angle_id`) | Strategy | Best stages | Guard |
|---|---|---|---|
| A-STATUS-QUO-COST | What staying the same is costing them, in time, money or stress | problem_aware | The cost has a source in `research/` or the brand files. No invented figures |
| A-BEFORE-AFTER | The same person or place, before and after | problem_aware, solution_aware | Real before and after only. Staged results are R-FACT |
| A-HIDDEN-STEP | The step most people skip, and what it changes | solution_aware | The step is real and the piece shows it |
| A-RECEIPT | Show the result itself: the number, the review, the screenshot | product_aware | Every figure has provenance |
| A-OBJECTION | Name the doubt that stops people, then answer it | product_aware | The objection comes from reviews, comments or research, quoted |
| A-MYTH | A common belief that is wrong, and the evidence | unaware, problem_aware | Evidence in `research/`. Pairs with H-CONTRARIAN |
| A-IDENTITY | "If you are this kind of person, this is for you" | unaware, problem_aware | The identity is specific, never a demographic label |
| A-HOW-IT-WORKS | The process explained simply | solution_aware | No jargon the audience does not use |
| A-COMPARISON | This way against the usual way, side by side | solution_aware, product_aware | Never name a competitor unless the brand allows it |
| A-FOUNDER-WHY | Why the person behind it started | product_aware | Genuine, specific story only |
| A-SOCIAL-PROOF | People like them already use it | product_aware, most_aware | Real customers, with permission |
| A-FIRST-STEP | The next step is small, easy or free | most_aware | The offer is real and in the brand files or the job |

## Rules

1. One angle per piece. The angle sentence in the brief must read as that strategy.
2. A series uses different angles across its pieces unless it is deliberately testing one angle.
3. If no angle fits, write `angle_id: A-OTHER` and say why in one line. Do not stretch one to fit.
