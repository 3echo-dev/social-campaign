# CTAs

The CTA is the one action the piece asks for.
Every post and script carries one `cta_id`, and the brief's CTA intent names it per platform.
Pick the CTA from the funnel stage and the awareness stage (see `awareness-stages.md`), not from what the brand would like most.
Industry tags are defined in `ad-styles.md`.

## CTAs

| Id (`cta_id`) | The action | Funnel stage | Example lines | Platform notes | Guard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTA-FOLLOW | Follow the account for more | TOFU | "Follow {brand} for part two." / "Follow for one {topic} tip a week." | tiktok: works best when a series is promised. instagram: pair with a reason, never a bare "follow us". facebook: weak; prefer CTA-SHARE | The promised series or cadence is real and planned |
| CTA-COMMENT-KEYWORD | Comment a keyword to get something | TOFU, MOFU | "Comment {keyword} and we'll send you {offer}." / "Type {keyword} below for the full list." | instagram: strongest here, when an auto-reply or a person answers every comment. tiktok: works, but replies are manual. facebook: works in groups | Someone or something answers every comment. The thing sent exists |
| CTA-SAVE | Save the post for later | TOFU, MOFU | "Save this for your next {situation}." / "Save it before you {task}." | instagram: the main ask for carousels and H-UTILITY. tiktok: use "favourite" in the caption only if the brand voice allows it. facebook: weak | The post is worth coming back to: a list, steps or a template |
| CTA-SHARE | Share or tag someone | TOFU | "Tag someone who {relatable situation}." / "Send this to the friend who {habit}." | all: strongest with H-QUESTION (relatable_callout) and myth_bust. facebook: share beats tag | Never ask people to tag strangers or spam. Not for regulated health or finance claims |
| CTA-LINK-BIO | Visit the link in bio | MOFU, BOFU | "{offer} is at the link in our bio." / "Full guide at the link in bio." | instagram and tiktok: the only link route in organic posts. facebook: use a real link in the post instead | The link is live, and the page matches the promise |
| CTA-BOOK | Book a call, visit or appointment | BOFU | "Book your {service} at {brand}, link in bio." / "Pick a time that suits you this week." | all: name what happens at the booking. Suits beauty-skincare, fitness-health, professional-services, hospitality-travel | Booking is open and the slots exist. No invented scarcity |
| CTA-DM | Send a message or DM | MOFU, BOFU | "DM us {keyword} and we'll reply with {offer}." / "Message {brand} with your question." | instagram and facebook: Messenger and DMs are native. tiktok: DMs are limited for new followers, so prefer CTA-COMMENT-KEYWORD | Someone answers DMs within the time the brand files state |
| CTA-JOIN | Sign up or join the community | MOFU | "Join {community} for free." / "Get on the list for {offer}." | facebook: groups work best here. instagram: broadcast channels. tiktok: point to the link in bio | The community or list exists and is active |
| CTA-BUY | Buy or shop now | BOFU | "Shop {product} at {brand}." / "Get yours before {real deadline}." | instagram and facebook: shop tags when the catalog is connected. tiktok: TikTok Shop only when the brand sells there | The product, price and any deadline are real and in the brand files or the job |
| CTA-FIRST-STEP | Start a free trial or take a small first step | BOFU | "Try {product} free for {trial length}." / "Start with {offer}, no card needed." | all: say the size of the step. Suits saas-ai-tech, education, fitness-health | The offer is real and in the brand files or the job. "No card needed" only when true |
| CTA-DOWNLOAD | Download a guide, app or file | MOFU | "Download {lead magnet}, link in bio." / "Get the {app} free on {store}." | all: tell people what they get before the link. Suits saas-ai-tech, education, finance | The file or app exists and is free if the line says so |

## Stage to CTA

| Stage | Reach for first | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| unaware | CTA-FOLLOW, CTA-SHARE, CTA-SAVE | CTA-BUY, CTA-BOOK |
| problem_aware | CTA-COMMENT-KEYWORD, CTA-SAVE, CTA-FOLLOW | CTA-BUY |
| solution_aware | CTA-SAVE, CTA-DOWNLOAD, CTA-COMMENT-KEYWORD | CTA-FOLLOW alone |
| product_aware | CTA-LINK-BIO, CTA-DM, CTA-FIRST-STEP | CTA-SHARE |
| most_aware | CTA-BUY, CTA-BOOK, CTA-FIRST-STEP | CTA-FOLLOW |

## Rules

1. One CTA per piece. Two asks means neither gets done.
2. The CTA is the last beat of a video and the last line of a caption. It may also sit on the end card.
3. Every offer, price, trial length and deadline is real and in the brand files or the job. An invented one is R-FACT.
4. Adapt the example lines to the brand's voice (see `voice.md`). Never paste them as written.
5. Write the CTA per platform. The same line on all three platforms means one of them is wrong.
