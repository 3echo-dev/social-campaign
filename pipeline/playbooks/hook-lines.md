# Hook lines

Example opening lines for each hook family and mechanism in `hooks.md`.
They are fill-in templates, not finished hooks: `write-hook` adapts them to the brand's voice and the anchor.
Each line is under 12 words.
The on-screen variant is shorter text for the first frame, where a video needs one.
Funnel is TOFU, MOFU or BOFU; "Does not fit" lists industry tags from `ad-styles.md` where the line reads wrong.

## Rules

1. The first 2 seconds are about the viewer or a tension, never a product slogan.
2. The product or brand name is never the first word of a hook.
3. Every number in a hook is real and sourced to `research/` or the brand files. An invented number is R-FACT.
4. Adapt every line to the brand's voice (see `voice.md`). Never paste a template as written.
5. The hook carries the `hook_family` and `hook_mechanism` of the row it came from, so results can be joined.
6. A line tagged "Does not fit" for the brand's industry needs a one-line reason before it is used.
