# Workflow: paid-ugc-campaign (v1.0.0)

Kinds: `paid_campaign`.
Same condition syntax and column meanings as `organic-post.md`. The media-buyer owns the job; the scriptwriter and the copywriter are support. Nothing here mutates a campaign: the proposal and the activation checklist are hand-off artifacts.
