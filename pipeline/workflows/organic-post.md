# Workflow: organic-post (v1.0.0)

Kinds: `organic_post`, `organic_series`, `ugc_creative`.
`plan-job.js` keeps a row when its Condition holds for the route. Conditions: `always`, `if:tag`, `unless:tag`, several joined by spaces (all must hold).
Tags: the route's disciplines (`research`, `strategy`, `ugc`, `video_intelligence`, `social_post`, `ads`), plus `media` (any deliverable that is not text only), `video_qa` (video_intelligence and media), `research_lite` (the route asked for one workstream, not four), `merged_publish` (no separate publish gate), `gate_<name>` for every gate the route kept, `paid`, `organic`.
Role is the agent's role name, or `-` for a script or a human row.
Owner is resolved per job: the agent named as the route's owner gets `owner`, any other specialist gets `support`.
