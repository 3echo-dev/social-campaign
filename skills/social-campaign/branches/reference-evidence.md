# Reference evidence that falls short

Read by `skills/social-campaign/SKILL.md`. Each section is applied exactly as written here.

## Reference evidence that falls short is asked once, never waited for

When the person asked for N reference videos (for example "the top 5 Reels in my niche"; N is 3 when they gave no number), or the job needs style references, read `research/competitors.md` before writing the brief and count only references that were verified, popular and in the job's niche. Off-niche results never count.
A rung of the discovery ladder that the file logs as `skipped: <reason>` counts as done. Never send the researcher back to run a skipped rung, and never send it back because the file blames a walled search, hashtag or profile page: the brief goes ahead with what was found.
If fewer than N were verified, the brief proceeds anyway. Its research line reads `References: n of N verified`, with the status of each link that was tried, and the blank slots stay blank.
Ask the person ONCE, without blocking the brief or the ideas: one `pipeline_board_ask` question on the board, asked without `blocking`, and the same line in chat.
Say in plain words how many were verified and give the counts, then list every single video link that could not be fetched and what each returned (for example "I could check 1 of the 5. I found 9 public video links, 1 downloaded and 8 could not be opened: this link returned a bot check, that one was removed"), never "Instagram and TikTok need a login" or any platform-wide login reason.
Offer "I'll paste links" (TikTok, Instagram and YouTube links are downloaded with `pipeline_reference_from_url`) and "Continue with what you found" (its description says plainly that the brief is written without watched references), with `allowText` true.
Collect every link that needed a sign-in or an upload into this one question; do not ask per video.
Do not wait for the answer before writing the brief or the ideas. Any answer without links, including "continue" or "you can look at any popular TikTok reels", means continue: it never re-runs the ladder or sends the researcher back to search again. Pasted links are downloaded and watched (`pipeline_reference_from_url`, then the teardown) and added to the brief's references when they arrive; nothing else is re-run.
The brief's "What you're deciding" section states plainly how many references were verified and that the rest were left blank.
