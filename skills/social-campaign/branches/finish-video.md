# Finishing the joined video

Read by `skills/social-campaign/SKILL.md`. Each section is applied exactly as written here.

## Finishing the joined video

The join of the clips is plain: no captions, no music, no on-screen text. All of that is added once, by `finish-video.py`, in one style (bold white on a dark box, inside the safe area above the bottom 500 px), and the finished video is what the person sees as the rough cut (`gate: "cut"`), already with its voice-over, music and captions. The order is fixed: sound plan, storyboard with its price, test clip, clips signed off, join, finishing, rough cut, post text, checks, final.

1. The sound plan is settled before the storyboard and recorded with `pipeline_finishing_choice` (`combine`): Music (from your library, upload a track, make with ElevenLabs which is priced with the clips, none), Voice-over (from your library, upload your recording, make with ElevenLabs from the script which is priced with the clips, none; it starts from the storyboard's `voice_over` answer) and Captions (from the voice-over words, from the script words, none). The library is one for the whole workspace, filterable by brand. The rough cut card has no combine rows. An older job whose cut request still carries the picks (a `submit_decision` with `reviewId` `cut`) is applied with `pipeline_decision_apply` right away, and the server records them in the same step.
2. After the clips are signed off with `pipeline_clips_signoff`, make what the price covers, join, then run `finish-video.py` and present the finished video as the rough cut. A change note on it that only asks for finishing ("add captions and background music") is not a redo: it records the new choice and stays a request for changes, so finish again and show the rough cut again. A note about a clip is a redo: the Creative Director rewrites that one prompt, the redo is made inside the allowance (two per clip, up to the extra credits shown) with no price tap, then sign off, join, finish and show again. A request that repeats something already decided is declined with `pipeline_board_request_decline` and `handled: true`, so the board shows "Already handled".
3. Only when no sound plan is recorded when finishing is about to run, ask ONE question with `pipeline_board_ask` ("Add captions", "Add background music", "Both", "Skip, use as is") and record it with `pipeline_finishing_choice` (pass `combine`, or captions, music, both, skip).
4. Captions: before running `finish-video.py` with captions, get the words that were actually spoken. Call `media_transcribe` on `media/D{n}/final-raw.mp4`; when it answers `needs_transcription_provider`, follow its steps (`creative_transcribe_audio` when ElevenLabs is connected, priced as a transcription item; `transcript_save` to keep it). Finishing builds the captions from that transcript. Without one it uses the script wording, and you must say so: "captions come from the script, not checked against the speech".
5. Music and voice-over made by ElevenLabs are priced with the clips and approved by the person's tap on the storyboard card, and that is the only way to make them. Music: `creative_generate_in_flow` with `node_type: "music"` and an `eleven_music` model, priced with `estimate_only: true`, saved as a `music` item with `pipeline_quote_save` before the storyboard is shown, made once after that tap (the spend guard lets only that call through, and only with an approved music price; pictures and video stay refused on this route). Voice-over: `creative_generate_speech` as for any voice line, with `generations_count` 2 or 3 for takes. Then `pipeline_combine_takes` saves the takes in the job and the library ("made with ElevenLabs", tagged with the brand); several takes are shown side by side on the board and the answer is recorded with `pipeline_combine_set`. Upload rows: ask with `pipeline_board_ask` (`wantsUpload` audio), then `pipeline_combine_set` with source `upload` and the path. Never create audio by hand, with Python or any other script; never say the video has music before a track is added.
6. After `finish-video.py` exits 0, present the finished video as the rough cut (`pipeline_review_present` with `gate: "cut"`, review copies as for any video) and wait. "Approve" lets the post text come next. There is no separate finished-video review. A change asked there reruns finishing only (change the recorded choice with `pipeline_finishing_choice` if the note says so, then run `finish-video.py` again): the clips and the text plan stay. Finishing also draws the post's on-screen text (`media/D{n}/onscreen.json`, saved by the join), so "Approve as is" still makes a finished video when the script has on-screen text.
7. Never re-join the clips yourself or run your own ffmpeg to finish. The stamped output of `finish-video.py` is the only finished video the board accepts.
